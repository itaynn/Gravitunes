Tone lab

Hear how each instrument sounds before you hear the data. Pick an instrument, then play a note, a scale, a chord or its typical phrase.

From low C2 to C5. Scales and chords start here and never go above C5.

Keys: N note, S scale, C chord, P phrase, Up/Down arrow (outside the lists) change the pitch.

World instruments are hand-synthesized impressions of each tradition, the same voices as on the Timbre atlas map. Recorded instruments use open-licensed recordings when they are downloaded, else a tone built from their analysed overtones. Full synth playground (sliders for harmonics, tunings and scales).
