Tone lab

Hear how each instrument sounds before you hear the data. Pick an instrument, then play a note, a scale, a chord or its typical phrase.

From C2 to A5. Scales and chords start here and never go above A5.

Keys: N note, S scale, C chord, P phrase, Up/Down arrow (outside the lists) change the pitch.

World instruments are the voices of the World orchestra map: recorded samples (open licences) where available, else synthesized impressions. Sampled instruments are the recordings themselves. Analysed recordings use open-licensed files when downloaded, else a tone built from their overtones. Full synth playground (sliders for harmonics, tunings and scales).
