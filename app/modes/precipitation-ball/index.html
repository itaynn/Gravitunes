01 / THE FIELD

Color becomes terrain.

Red excess and blue excess over green are added into a single height field; black is flat. The negative height gradient gives the downhill acceleration vector, so steeper slopes accelerate the ball more strongly.

02 / THE MOTION

Stillness, then drift.

The ball has no gravity or self-propelled movement. On a raised slope, it is continuously accelerated downhill; surface friction controls how quickly its momentum fades.

03 / THE SOURCE

One moving planet.

Based on NASA's IMERG precipitation visualization. This prerecorded animation depicts rain and snowfall—not temperature.
