# Ozone probe

**Status:** merged into Explore (`app/modes/explore/`)

## What you hear
Move the mouse or arrow keys over the map and hear the value under the cursor.

## Mapping
- Pitch: value, quantized to a scale. Harmonics: how much the neighborhood varies. Pan: longitude.
- Screen reader: "Ozone 42 ppbv near 30°N, 80°E".
