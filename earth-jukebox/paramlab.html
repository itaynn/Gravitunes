Press any ▶ to start sound.

01 / ONE AT A TIME

Each ▶ solos that parameter: only it changes, and everything else plays a plain reference (pure tone, middle C, quarter notes, C major). From and To are the Sound lab's range for that row: when the row is driven by the map, the sound moves between them; when it's manual, only From is used. Sweep moves From → To → From over 8 seconds. List parameters can also cycle through every option, two bars each.

02 / ALL TOGETHER

The In the mix switch on each card decides what that parameter does when you press Play all together: Off keeps the reference, From or To holds that end, and Sweep moves it, the way a storm moving across the map would. All sweeping parameters move together, like one storm. Changes are heard right away while it plays.

03 / CANDIDATES: COMPARE AND DECIDE

Save the current settings (rain, snow, global sound and the mix) as a candidate. Press ▶ on another candidate while one plays to switch instantly, A/B style. Candidates are saved in this browser; use Copy choices to share one with the team.

    Send to Sound lab writes the rain and snow sounds (key, scale, ranges, instrument, chord, notes) and the global sound (voices, tempo, glide, reverb, blend, volume, strict tuning) into the Sound lab's saved settings. What drives each row (X, Y, color…) is kept. Reload the Sound lab page to hear it there.
