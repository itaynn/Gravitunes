01 / THE FIELD

Color carries a pulse.

Red and blue mark raised areas; black is flat. The negative height gradient points downhill, away from a colored peak, and sets the direction of each push.

02 / THE MOTION

Stillness, then drift.

The ball has no gravity or self-propelled movement. It stays put until the raised red/blue surface reaches it, then glides downhill with long-lasting inertia.

03 / THE SOURCE

One moving planet.

Based on NASA's IMERG precipitation visualization. This prerecorded animation depicts rain and snowfall—not temperature.
